# WTF

The reader lost the thread on your last message. Give it back in a form they can act on, without asking them to re-read the original.

1. **Find the sticking point.** `$ARGUMENTS` names it; with no arguments, the whole last message is the target. Say in one line what you are restating.
2. **Restate in the register.** Invoke the `writing-clearly-and-concisely` skill (via the Skill tool) and use its compact prose register. One line of context, then the point in one sentence that stands alone, then at most five short beats. Keep the real terms the reader needs to search or discuss the topic; define each in **bold** the moment it is used.
3. **Draw it when the shape is the point.** A flow, a call path, a file layout, a before-and-after: invoke the `show-me` skill (via the Skill tool) and pick its smallest chat view — pseudocode, tree, or diff. Chat only; no files, no HTML.
4. **Teach it when the concept is new.** If the reader does not know the underlying idea at all — not your message, the thing itself — invoke the `eli5` skill (via the Skill tool) on that concept and let it take over.

Done means: the reader can act on the restatement alone. Stop there; no summary, no menu of next topics.
